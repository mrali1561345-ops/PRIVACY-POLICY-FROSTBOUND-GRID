# PRIVACY POLICY FOR FROSTBOUND GRID
Last updated: September 30, 2026

Frostbound Grid is built as a completely offline puzzle game. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and confirms our strict zero-data collection policy.

# 1. ZERO DATA COLLECTION
Frostbound Grid does not collect, store, share, or process any personal information, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or usage statistics. You can play the entire game without registering, logging in, or providing any personal details.

# 2. NO THIRD-PARTY SDKs OR ADS
Our application contains:
- NO third-party advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads)
- NO analytics or tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics)
- NO social media integration plugins
- NO background tracking services

# 3. 100% OFFLINE PLAY & LOCAL STORAGE
Frostbound Grid operates 100% offline and does not require an internet connection to function. All game progress—including unlocked stages, earned stars, and audio settings—is saved strictly on your local device storage. This data is never transmitted to any external server or third party.

# 4. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY
Because Frostbound Grid does not collect any data or use any online features, it is 100% safe for players of all ages, including children under 13, and fully complies with COPPA and GDPR regulations.

# 5. DATA DELETION
Since all game progress is stored locally on your mobile device, uninstalling the app or clearing the app cache via your device settings will permanently delete all local game data.
